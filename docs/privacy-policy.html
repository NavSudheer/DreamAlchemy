🔮 Dream Alchemy - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

🔮 Introduction

Dream Alchemy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Dream Alchemy mobile application (the "App").

📱 Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Dream Descriptions: Text you enter for analysis
  • Support Information: Contact details and message content you choose to provide when requesting support
  • No App Account: This release does not create a Dream Alchemy account

Automatically Collected:

  • Trial Tracking: Analysis count, trial start date (stored locally)
  • Subscription Processing: Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and entitlement metadata needed to provide subscriptions

🛡️ How We Use Your Information

Primary Uses:

  • Dream Analysis: Process your dream text through AI services
  • Subscription Management: Track trial usage and subscription status
  • Customer Support: Respond to inquiries and technical issues

AI Processing:

  • Dream text is sent over HTTPS to the configured AI service for analysis
  • Analysis results are returned and stored locally on your device
  • We do not use dream content to train our own models; the configured provider's processing and retention terms apply

💳 Subscription & Payment Information

Trial System:

  • Local Tracking: Trial usage (3 analyses + 7 days) stored on your device
  • No Payment Required: Trial does not require payment information
  • Fair Usage: Trial limits prevent abuse while protecting user privacy

Subscription Processing:

  • Apple App Store: All subscription billing handled by Apple
  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat for subscription management
  • No Card Storage: We never store payment card information
  • Subscription Data: RevenueCat and Apple process purchase, receipt, product, and entitlement metadata needed to provide and restore subscriptions

🔒 Data Security

Encryption:

  • App requests to our configured services use encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Journal, analysis, trial, and saved-item data is stored in the app's local device storage
  • Our application backend does not intentionally create a permanent dream-content database; service-provider processing and retention are governed by their applicable policies

Third-Party Services:

  • AI Analysis: Encrypted transmission to the configured AI service for dream analysis
  • RevenueCat: Subscription and entitlement processing under RevenueCat's applicable privacy terms
  • Apple Services: App Store policies apply for payment processing

📊 Data Sharing

We DO NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share dream content with advertisers
  • Use your dreams for marketing purposes
  • Store dream text permanently on our servers

Limited Sharing:

  • AI Services: Dream text is sent to the configured AI service to produce the requested analysis; provider processing and retention terms apply
  • Legal Requirements: Only when required by law

👤 Your Rights

Access & Control:

  • Local Data: View and manage supported journal, analysis, and saved-item data in the app
  • Delete Local Data: Delete supported records in the app or remove app data through device controls
  • No App Account: This release does not create a Dream Alchemy account or synced account profile
  • Provider Requests: Contact us for help identifying the relevant provider process for an applicable privacy request

Subscription Rights:

  • Cancel Anytime: Manage subscriptions through App Store
  • Refund Policy: Apple's refund policy applies
  • Restore Purchases: Request restoration through Apple and RevenueCat where supported for the Apple ID used for purchase

🧒 Children's Privacy

Dream Alchemy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

🔮 Dream Analysis Specifics

How Dream Analysis Works:

  1. You enter dream text in the app
  2. Text is sent over HTTPS to the configured AI analysis service
  3. Analysis results returned and displayed
  4. No permanent storage of dream content on our servers
  5. Results stored locally on your device only

Your Dream Privacy:

  • Processing: Our application backend does not intentionally persist dream text; provider processing and retention terms apply
  • Local Storage: Full analysis stored only on your device
  • Automated Processing: Dream analyses are generated automatically; service-provider safety, support, and legal processes may still apply under their policies
  • No Profiling: We don't create personality profiles from your dreams

For privacy questions or requests:

  • Email: support@dreamalchemy.app
  • Subject Line: "Privacy Policy Inquiry"
  • Response: We will review privacy requests and respond as required by applicable law

Depending on your location, privacy laws may provide rights concerning access, deletion, correction, or restriction. Contact us to submit a request. Relevant frameworks may include:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

  • Apple App Store privacy disclosures and platform requirements
